How to play

Goal
Push every box onto a circle.
Move
Arrow keys or WASD, or the on-screen arrows. Hold a key or arrow to repeat.
Walk
Click or tap a free cell and the worker walks there.
Push once
Tap a box right next to the worker.
Move a box
Drag a box to a cell. The worker walks over and pushes it there.
Undo
Undo button, U, Backspace, or right-click. A walk or a box move undoes in one step.
Restart
Restart button or R.
Sound
The Muted / Sound on button in the top bar.
Colors
Red flash: you can't move there. While dragging a box, the cell under your finger is green if the box can get there, red if not.
